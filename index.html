How are these numbers calculated?

Tokens, multiplied by an estimated energy cost per token (around 0.0015 Wh from Patterson et al. 2021 and Luccioni et al. 2023), give the compute energy. Cooling water is a separate empirical estimate, around 500 ml per 250 tokens, based on AP News and UC Riverside reporting on data-centre water use.

Both numbers are indicative, not billing-grade. The point is the order of magnitude. CO₂ depends heavily on which grid is powering the data centre. Water is more direct, and lands harder.

03 · Why bother

Bad prompts aren't just annoying. They're expensive.

~3×

Token spend

A vague prompt triggers 3 to 5 correction cycles. Each one re-sends your full context. Token cost compounds, even when each individual call is cheap.

500ml

Water per chat

A short ChatGPT conversation can cost ~500ml of cooling water. Microsoft's data-centre water use grew 34% in 2022. AI is a thirsty hobby.

$5K

Already happening

The fine one lawyer paid for citing AI-hallucinated cases in court. Recruiters, doctors, election callers, all sanctioned for AI output gone wrong. Real consequences, already here, not future tense.
