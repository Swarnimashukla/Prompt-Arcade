What is being scored

Six properties, not six opinions.

Each one maps to a failure that has actually happened in production systems: an orchestrator that quietly became the overloaded agent, an irreversible action with nobody watching, prose handoffs that destroyed provenance, and a loop that nobody gave a terminal state. Getting all six is possible with four agents.
